## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: High-ticket GEO positioning

The public landing page SHALL position KORA as a high-ticket GEO and atendimento automation consultancy for Brazilian SMEs, not as a site subscription provider.

#### Scenario: Visitor sees the first viewport
- **WHEN** a visitor lands on the home page
- **THEN** the primary headline and supporting copy communicate AI search visibility, GEO, and being found, cited, and chosen by generative engines
- **AND** the primary CTA points to the diagnostic application instead of WhatsApp or site plans

#### Scenario: Visitor scans the navigation
- **WHEN** the visitor reads the header navigation
- **THEN** there is no "Planos" or site-pricing navigation item
- **AND** there is a diagnostic-focused CTA.

### Requirement: Low-ticket site signals removed

The public conversion narrative SHALL remove low-ticket subscription signals.

#### Scenario: Public page is searched for old offer copy
- **WHEN** public-facing content is searched
- **THEN** it does not invite the visitor to create a site, choose a site plan, buy hosting/domain bundles, or request unlimited site changes.

### Requirement: GEO category education

The landing page SHALL explain GEO in practical language for business owners.

#### Scenario: Visitor does not know GEO
- **WHEN** the visitor reaches the category or FAQ content
- **THEN** the page explains that GEO prepares a brand to be understood, cited, and recommended by AI answer engines
- **AND** it differentiates GEO from SEO without claiming SEO is obsolete.
